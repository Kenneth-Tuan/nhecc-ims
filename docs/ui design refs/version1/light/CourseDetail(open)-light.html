About this Course

This course explores the spiritual depth of John's Gospel, focusing on the seven "I AM" statements of Jesus. We will dive deep into the theological significance and practical application of these truths in our daily lives.

Whether you are new to the Bible or have been studying for years, this class offers a welcoming environment for questions and discussion.

target Target Audience

  • New believers seeking foundation
  • Small group leaders
  • Anyone hungry for the Word

Requirements

Please bring your own Bible and a notebook. Study materials will be provided digitally via the church app.
